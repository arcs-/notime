## Promotional text

Pick the apps and websites that take your time. notime blocks them and makes you wait before each opening. The wait grows every time you open one that day.

## Description

notime blocks the apps and websites you choose. When you open one, you see a shield with a single button. The first tap starts a short wait. The second tap, after the wait, opens the app for twelve minutes. Then it is blocked again.

Each opening in a day makes the next wait longer. The wait and the open time are settings you can change. A small chart shows how often you ran into the shield.

There is nothing else. No account, no subscription, no internet, no tracking. Your choices and your counts stay on your phone.

notime uses Apple's Screen Time. It never sees which apps or sites you picked.

## Review notes

notime is a personal app and website blocker built on the Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity). It asks for individual Screen Time authorization on first launch and has no family or child features.

To test: grant Screen Time access, pick an app such as Safari or a website in the picker, then open it. A shield appears with one button, "Open for 12 min". Tap it once to start the wait, tap again after the wait to open the app. The app is blocked again twelve minutes later.

The app has no accounts, no server and no network access. Nothing is collected.
