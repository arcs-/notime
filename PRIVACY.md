# notime privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

notime is a free app and website blocker for iPhone. It does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What the app does with your data

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
- **No analytics or tracking.** The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs and no third-party code.
- **No network access.** The app never contacts a server.
- **Screen Time.** notime uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to shield the apps and websites you choose. Apple gives the app opaque tokens for your choices, not their names, and the app never sees which apps or sites they are. Authorization is for your own device only; notime does not manage other family members.
- **Local storage only.** Your selection, your settings and the daily counts shown on the Stats screen are stored on your device in the app's private container. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Children

notime is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
